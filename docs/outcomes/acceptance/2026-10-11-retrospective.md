## FW-002 — Private family gift coordination

Accepted capability: invited family members can maintain wishlists and coordinate
gift buying while reservation and purchase details remain absent from the
signed-in recipient's own wishlist view.

Accepted criteria:

1. An invited member can sign in to an existing wishlist, including wishes added
   before their first visit.
2. Family gift givers can reserve a wish and mark it bought without removing it
   from the wishlist.
3. The signed-in recipient does not receive reservation or purchase details for
   their own wishes.

Historical delivery: v1.0.0, published 7 September 2026. The historical family
walkthrough, core-release acceptance and recipient-privacy tests remain the
supporting evidence linked in FW-002. Anonymous guest gift buying is excluded.

## FW-003 — Optional automatic household updates

Accepted capability: a household can opt into automatic published improvements
while retaining its wishlist data and settings, with manual updating still
available.

Accepted criteria:

1. Automatic updates are optional; a household can retain manual control and both
   routes follow published releases.
2. An opted-in household receives and serves a published improvement without
   someone manually starting that update.
3. Updating preserves existing family records and installation settings; a failed
   build leaves the previous site available and can be retried.

Historical delivery: v1.1.1, published 3 October 2026. Acceptance is within the
recorded test scope: a disposable household, synthetic data, observed scheduled
delivery and a tested failed-build recovery. It does not promise punctual updates,
every possible rollback, or a new-account installation walkthrough.
