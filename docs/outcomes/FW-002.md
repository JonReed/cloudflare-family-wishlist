## Delivered and accepted

Delivered in v1.0.0, published 7 September 2026. Jon confirmed this outcome and
its three success criteria as delivered and accepted on 11 October 2026. This is
a retrospective record written on that date; it does not imply the outcome ID
or this wording existed at the time of delivery.

## Why this mattered

A family needs somewhere to collect gift ideas and avoid competing purchases,
without showing a person what others plan to buy them. Members can help maintain
each other's lists; the recipient's view excludes their own gift coordination data.

## What the history establishes

The 5 September existing-account walkthrough records organiser and invited-member
sign-in, preservation of wishes created before first sign-in, and a purchase that
retained the wish. The release-era service tests explicitly cover recipient privacy
and competing gift-giver permissions. Test source shows the assertions that exist;
it is not, on its own, a record of a passing run.

The 7 September readiness record says the maintainer accepted the completed
walkthroughs for the core release, with stated limits. Version 1.0.0 was published
that evening. This is stronger evidence than a release label alone, but the three
criteria above are a retrospective formulation, not an original signed-off brief.

## Boundary

This outcome concerns admitted family members. At this release, external sharing
links were read-only and omitted buying information. Anonymous guest gift buying
belongs to FW-001 and still awaits Jon's QA; it is not covered by this history.

The record does not claim a complete fresh-account installation walkthrough or
unattended updates. It does not measure reduced duplicate purchases or family
satisfaction. No original target date has been reconstructed from the release date.
