# Your family's wishlist

Keep ideas in one place throughout the year, and coordinate presents without spoiling the surprise.
This guide is for a family whose wishlist is already installed. Setting one up? Start with the
[installation guide](DEPLOYMENT.md).

## Join your family

Open the address your organiser sends you. Sign in with the exact email address they invited and
the one-time code from Cloudflare Access. There is no separate wishlist password or public sign-up.
Your organiser can prepare your list before you first sign in.

Choose **My wishlist** or another person's gift tag to switch lists. Everyone admitted to your
family space can add, edit and remove wishes on every list. Treat it as a shared family notebook,
not a private diary.

## Add an idea

Add a wish on the list you want it to appear on. A name is enough: a shop link, picture, notes,
approximate price in pounds and priority are optional. High-priority wishes appear first.
Experiences and homemade gifts belong here just as much as products.

Have a product link? Use **Fill from link**, then check the suggested name, picture and price before
saving. You can change any suggestion. Lookup does not buy anything or save a wish automatically.

When you share a shop page using **Add from anywhere**, the draft opens before product lookup
finishes. A fetching message explains what is happening. The fields receiving product details and
the save button wait for lookup, but you can write **Anything else to know?**, choose a priority and
select the family lists to add it to. Choose **Enter details myself** to stop waiting and fill in the
product fields yourself; a late result will not overwrite your work.

The **Add from anywhere** page explains the optional phone shortcuts and desktop browser button.
You can also just copy a link into the wishlist. If a shop blocks lookup or gives incomplete details,
manual entry still works. Core forms work without JavaScript; automatic background lookup needs it.

## Plan a gift without spoiling it

On someone else's list:

- Choose **I’ll get this** to tell the rest of the family you are handling that gift.
- Choose **Mark as bought** once you have bought it.
- Made a mistake? **Mark as not bought** keeps your reservation but undoes the purchase status.
- Changed your mind? **Cancel claim** releases it for someone else.

Other family members can see these buying plans. The person whose wishlist it is cannot: their
page does not receive that information. You cannot reserve a gift on your own list.

**See where to find it** opens the saved shop link. Buying and payment happen at the shop, not in
Family Wishlist. Marking a gift bought does not remove the wish; remove it later if it is no longer
wanted. Avoid putting secret buying plans in the wish's notes, which its owner can read.

## Edit or remove a wish

Open **Edit this wish** beside the item and choose the edit or remove option. Editing opens a form;
save your changes or cancel to leave the wish unchanged. With JavaScript enabled, the dialog asks
before discarding an unsaved edit. Without it, the controls open inline.

Removal asks for confirmation and cannot be undone in the app. It removes the wish and its buying
plan, so check that you are removing the right item.

## Share ideas outside the family

Choose **Share this list**, give the viewing link a name you will recognise, and copy it to send.
Anyone with that link can view that person's wishes without signing in, but cannot edit them or
see who is buying anything. The link's name is a reminder for your family, not a restriction on who
can open or forward it. Share it only where you are comfortable sharing the list's contents.

Each wishlist can have up to five active links. In **Profile**, the family can review and stop
sharing existing links. Stopping one makes that link stop working without affecting the others.
It cannot erase copies or screenshots someone has already made.

## Your profile and family

Use **Profile** to change the name your family sees and manage viewing links. Read the sign-out
notice: Cloudflare Access sign-out can also sign you out of other applications in the same Access
account, not just the wishlist.

The organiser uses **Family** to invite people by their exact email address. The wishlist does not
send invitation emails; the organiser shares the address and instructions directly. This organiser
role controls admission, not exclusive ownership of everybody's lists.

If sign-in fails, check you used the invited email address and ask your organiser to check access.
For hosting or update problems, the organiser can use the
[backup and upgrade guide](BACKUP_RESTORE_UPGRADE.md) and
[project support information](STEWARDSHIP.md).
